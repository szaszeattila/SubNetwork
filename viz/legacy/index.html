Protein assemblies — several proteins, one receptorSeveral proteins ChEMBL records as one physical assembly. Collapsed, they merge into one hexagon; hulled, every subunit stays on the canvas inside a dashed enclosure — and either way the shared-target ranking counts the assembly once, not once per subunit. Nothing that is not a subunit is ever enclosed: a non-member that the layout puts inside is cut out of the enclosure and the outline drawn around it. Which proteins form one assembly comes from ChEMBL's own target typing, never from gene names. The panel's grouping control sets this per tier or per assembly, and can turn it off entirely.
